description: >-
  systementwurf.org is a continuously developed teaching and demonstration
  platform created to support the System Design I lecture and exercise series
  in aerospace engineering.

  The platform was designed to provide students with a more interactive and
  intuitive understanding of complex systems engineering concepts such as
  redundancy, safety assessment, distributed architectures, avionics,
  communication, fault handling, and software design.

  Besides providing lecture-related material, the demonstrator includes
  interactive examples, visualizations, simulations, and practical exercises
  intended to bridge the gap between theoretical systems engineering concepts
  and their implementation in real aerospace systems.

  The project evolved continuously between 2021 and 2026 and was used in
  lectures, exercises, and student supervision activities at the Institute of
  Aircraft Systems (ILS), University of Stuttgart.
