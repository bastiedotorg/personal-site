description: >-
  Quaduino is an Arduino-based educational demonstrator implementing an
  all-active quadruplex computing architecture inspired by fault-tolerant
  avionics systems. The platform was developed to provide students with a
  practical and approachable introduction to redundancy strategies,
  distributed voting mechanisms, fault detection, and resilient system behavior.

  The demonstrator consists of four cooperating processing nodes executing
  synchronized logic while continuously exchanging state information.
  Fault scenarios such as node failures, delayed communication, or inconsistent
  outputs can be injected and analyzed during operation.

  Quaduino is used within the HYMASY hybrid laboratory environment at the
  Institute of Aircraft Systems (ILS), University of Stuttgart, where it
  supports teaching and experimentation related to integrated modular avionics,
  dependable systems, and distributed architectures.

  Besides its educational purpose, the project also serves as a lightweight
  experimentation platform for redundancy management concepts and
  fault-tolerant coordination strategies.
