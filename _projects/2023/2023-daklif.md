description: >-
  DaKliF builds on the results of PAFA-ONE, TALIA, and INTELWI and further
  develops Plug-and-Fly concepts for CS-25 avionics platforms, in particular
  Integrated Modular Avionics (IMA) systems.

  The project addresses the high static configuration effort of today’s
  avionics platforms by exploring model-based and automated configuration
  methods. Central contributions include self-description mechanisms for
  avionics modules and peripherals, advanced virtualization concepts,
  automatic detection and configuration, and the extension of ARINC 653-based
  platform concepts.

  DaKliF uses the Open Avionics Architecture Model (OAAM) as a central data
  backbone for describing avionics architectures and configuration-relevant
  information. The goal is to enable avionics platforms that can use system
  resources more efficiently and adaptively while reducing development and
  configuration effort.

  My work in DaKliF focuses on OAAM-based configuration, TSN/SDN networking,
  device self-description, automatic network configuration, and the integration
  of heterogeneous avionics components into a consistent model-based
  engineering workflow.
